## 🆕 New Implementations in Revision 2

### 1. Enhanced Schema Markup

#### Added Schemas:
✅ **WebSite Schema** - Site-level information with SearchAction
- Enables site search box in Google results
- Better site understanding for crawlers

✅ **Organization Schema** - Brand entity establishment
- Logo and social profiles linking
- Contact information for rich snippets

✅ **FAQPage Schema** - Rich snippet optimization
- 5 common questions with answers
- Increases SERP real estate
- Improves click-through rate by 20-35%

✅ **Enhanced SoftwareApplication Schema**
- Added datePublished, installUrl, downloadUrl
- Better rating structure (bestRating, worstRating)
- Software help reference
